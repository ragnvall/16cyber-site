How to buy

How to buy

Three steps. The first is an email to us; the official order happens inside NASA's system.

  1. 1

    Send us what you need

    Send sewp@16cyber.com what you need, how many, your agency and your deadline. We reply with a ballpark quote you can use to get budget approved.

  2. 2

    Your contracting officer sends the official request

    They send it through NASA's SEWP Quote Request Tool. We answer there, inside NASA's system, and your agency picks.

  3. 3

    We deliver and support

    When your agency chooses 16 Cyber, we deliver the order and support what we supplied.

About fair opportunity

Orders over $15,000 go to every Category A holder through NASA's tool, so each one gets a fair chance to bid. We can quote only items already in SEWP's product database.

Contract clause A.1.13: “Contractors will be provided a fair opportunity at the individual order level as appropriate per FAR Part 16.505(b), including the SEWP RFQ tools.” The same clause requires that 16 Cyber “shall not market, quote or otherwise offer for sale, any IT Solutions not listed under this contract, until the said solutions are included in the SEWP database of record, and available to all Government end-users.”

Problem with an order? Email sewp@16cyber.com. Or call [SEWP phone number].
