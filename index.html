About the vehicle

What NASA SEWP VI means for agencies working with 16 Cyber

SEWP VI is NASA's government-wide acquisition contract for information technology products and product-based services, giving federal agencies a streamlined, compliant path to IT, communications, and AV solutions, including cloud and cybersecurity capabilities.

As a Category A holder, 16 Cyber sits within the group covering the full range of IT, communications, and AV products and product-based services, including installation, integration, testing, training, and maintenance.

  • Government-wide reach

    Any U.S. federal agency can place orders through SEWP VI, not NASA alone.

  • Multi-award, fair opportunity

    SEWP VI is a multi-award GWAC. Orders are placed through a fair-opportunity process at the individual order level, consistent with FAR Part 16.505(b), using the SEWP RFQ system.

  • Category A scope

    IT, communications, and AV products and product-based services, including cloud and cybersecurity tools.
